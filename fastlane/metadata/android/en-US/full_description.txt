OpenCaller identifies and blocks spam, scam, and robocalls — entirely on your phone.

The spam database lives on your device. Lookups happen offline in microseconds while the call rings. No account, no ads, and no server ever sees who calls you.

WHAT IT DOES
• Screens incoming calls against a signed database of reported spam numbers (built weekly from public government complaint data: US FTC and FCC)
• Silences or rejects spam by category — you decide per category
• Detects spoofed callers with no database at all: numbers faking your own prefix, your own number, or structurally impossible numbers — works in every country
• Personal rules: block or always-allow numbers, prefixes (+1800*), and SMS sender names (like promo gateways)
• Optional: warnings for WhatsApp calls from reported numbers, spam-SMS warning/muting, and a large on-screen warning badge — each behind its own opt-in permission
• Weekly database updates you control: Wi-Fi only, any network, or off

WHAT IT REFUSES TO DO
• No contacts access — ever. We cannot upload your address book because we never ask for it.
• No accounts, no analytics, no trackers, no ads.
• No "see who viewed your profile". No name lookup of strangers — that feature only exists in apps that harvest everyone's contact books. That's the practice this app exists to reject.
• It will not become your default dialer or SMS app. Your calls and messages stay in the apps you chose.

HOW UPDATES STAY TRUSTWORTHY
Every database file is Ed25519-signed; the app refuses anything that does not verify against its pinned key, and refuses rollbacks to older data. The download server needs no trust at all.

The permission list is the privacy policy. Everything is open source and verifiable.
